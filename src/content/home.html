Ethical and Responsible Innovation

Academic and Industry partners

We work within a network of universities and SMEs in Estonia, Greece and the Netherlands to stimulate ethical and accessible innovation in reproductive health care. Our extended network involves the Genetics Lab of the National Health System in Greece (ESY) at G.N.A Alexandra Hospital.

Our new research initiatives

Research is the backbone of innovation. NESTOR partners are committed to pushing the boundaries of knowledge. We focus on four main areas: non-invasive prenatal diagnosis, non-invasive IVF tools, uterine health and reproductive life-span prediction.

Secondee Community

Beyond the confines of traditional research, NESTOR is deeply rooted in community development. We accomplish this by engaging researchers in our network and equipping them with interdisciplinary training that transcends academia and industry.
